<p align="center"><b>Hypixel SkyBlock Flipping Toolkit</b><br>
Free, local and open source. It reads the market and your profile, and shows the flips that fit <i>you</i>.</p>

---

HyFlip ranks forge, Bazaar, craft and NPC flips for your own account: your HOTM level, unlocked recipes, forge
slots, real forge speed, purse and the time until your next session. It warns about manipulated or thin markets and
tells you why a flip may not work.

It only reads and shows. It never sends anything to the game, never clicks, buys or sells for you.

## Features

- **Flip Calc:** splits a budget across all four kinds of flips for the time you have.
- **Forge, Bazaar, Craft and NPC Flippers:** ranked lists with costs, fill times, liquidity and alerts.
- **Forge Manager:** your slots' timers and a plan for the free ones.
- **Journal and backtest:** what you earned against the estimate, and how good the estimates are.
- **Item icons** from the Hypixel SkyBlock resource pack you already have.

## Install

Download the latest version from [Releases](../../releases):

| System | File |
| --- | --- |
| Windows | `HyFlip_x.y.z_x64-setup.exe` or `.msi` |
| Linux | `.AppImage` or `.deb` |

HyFlip needs **Java 17 or newer** (any vendor). On Windows the installer is not signed yet, so SmartScreen may
ask you to confirm the first time ("More info" → "Run anyway").

Then open **Settings** to add your Hypixel API key and Minecraft name, and optionally load the resource pack for
item icons.
